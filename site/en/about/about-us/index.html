About Us

Our Mission

We support the sustainable development of businesses and society through mathematical technology.

  • Improving productivity and creating a comfortable working environment for businesses through streamlining operations.
  • Realizing a sustainable society and environment through the optimal utilization of resources.

CEO Message

We are pleased to announce the establishment of MOAI Lab Inc., with the aim of contributing to solving challenges in industry and society through the integration of mathematical optimization and machine learning technologies.

Our members, as experts in operations research, have been promoting academic research in this field and related fields for many years. We also have a proven track record of actively applying these results to practical applications, streamlining operations, optimizing processes, and supporting decision-making in business settings. Based on this knowledge and experience, we will continue to promote further technological innovation to provide more practical and effective solutions to the increasingly large and complex challenges of today. Our mission is to support sustainable growth and efficient resource utilization by accurately analyzing the challenges faced by businesses and society and proposing optimal solutions.

MOAI Lab Inc. aims to be a reliable partner for our customers, utilizing mathematical approaches and cutting-edge technologies to explore new possibilities together.

Koji Nonobe, Representative Director
